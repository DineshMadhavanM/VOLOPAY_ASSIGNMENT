Slide 2 • UX Architecture & Deliberate Omissions

2. Interface Choices & What Was Deliberately Left Out

Manager-Centric Design Principles: Prioritize high-signal exceptions, explicit denominators, and instant evidence drill-down over vanity metrics.

  • Built-In Replay Simulator Toolbar: Features 5 instant buttons to verify idempotent repeat imports, manual route overrides, and late-arriving update batches directly in the UI.
  • Batch JSON Contract Verification: Evaluator-friendly JSON upload dropzone to verify arbitrary incoming batches against DATA_CONTRACT.md schema and idempotency rules.
  • Strict Denominator Transparency: Distinguishes Call Reps from Meeting Organisers (organisers are not assumed to be AEs or attendees) and measures transcript coverage against all 44 selected records.
  • Deep Evidence Drawer: Allows clicking any interaction to inspect formatted turns, elapsed timecodes, verbatim quotes, and honest limitations.
Deliberate Omissions:
  • Zero Fabricated Summaries: For the 26 records with null audio, we deliberately declined to generate fake summaries. They are transparently logged as Intake / No Audio Supplied.
  • No Date Picker: All supplied records occur on 21 August 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). A date filter provides no analytical value and was omitted.

Slide 3 • Routing Architecture & Difficult Decision

3. Routing Taxonomy & The Difficult Case (M07)

Action-Oriented Routing Architecture: Categorizes interactions into 6 distinct operational destinations:

1. Deal Next Steps / Pipeline
High-intent opportunities (M03 Asterloom Systems, C03 Milan API, C08 Raghav referral).
2. Customer Success / Escalations
Urgent customer bug escalations & migrations (M07 Willowfield Trust, M04 Cedarwave).
3. Partnerships / Channel Advisory
Incorporation advisory channels (C07 Willowbank Advisory, M01 Harborpath).
4. Vendor & Internal Enablement
Vendor procurement (M02 LedgerBridge) and staff training (M10 Billing walkthrough).
The Difficult Routing Decision — Willowfield Trust (M07):
Initially arrived with no transcript and was provisionally labeled as a routine check-in. In Update Batch 2 (Rev 2), 27 turns were supplied showing Customer A screen-sharing critical software defects (payment description edits reverting upon save; recreate payment file upload failing). Shifted immediately to Customer Success / Bug Escalation. Under our data contract, a manager override survives batch replays and browser reloads.

Slide 5 • Engineering Ethics & AI Disclosure

5. Trade-offs, Rejected Decision & Next Steps

The Deliberately Rejected Decision: Auto-Estimating Deal Pipeline ARR
Alternative Considered: Using an LLM to hallucinate contract values based on prospect employee count (e.g. estimating Asterloom at $45,000 ARR).
Why Rejected: The prompt strictly prohibits inventing pipeline value or deal outcomes. Speculative revenue figures destroy executive trust during audits.
Accepted Trade-off: We report qualitative commercial intent and verbatim financial statements (e.g. Asterloom's INR 26–39 lakh monthly vendor spend) without guessing ARR.

AI Implementation & Scope Disclosure:

  • Pre-generated vs. Dynamic: Conversation analyses and evidence citations were prepared beforehand. Batch ingestion, revision deduplication, manual override persistence, and ZIP manifest generation run completely dynamically client-side.
  • Approximate Time Spent: ~7 hours total (modeling, UI design, contract compliance, documentation).
  • Known Limitations & Future Scope: Cross-call speaker identity is not unified upstream. Future release will integrate live webhooks for automated recording synchronization.
